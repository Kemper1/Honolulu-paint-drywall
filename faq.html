Do you take small jobs?

expand_more

Absolutely. We understand that sometimes you just need a single wall patched or a small room refreshed. Chad personally handles smaller repair and paint jobs with the same dedication to quality as full-home renovations.

How do you price jobs?

expand_more

We believe in complete transparency. Every project begins with a free, detailed walkthrough. We then provide a formalized, itemized quote breaking down labor and materials. There are no surprises or hidden fees — the price you see is the price you pay to get the job done right.

How soon can you start?

expand_more

Our schedule fluctuates based on current projects, but we typically aim to begin new work within 1 to 3 weeks of quote approval. For emergency drywall repairs, we try to accommodate faster timelines whenever possible.

Do you supply materials?

expand_more

Yes, we provide all the necessary professional-grade supplies, including premium paints, professional drywall compounds, and protective coverings. If you have a specific custom paint or finish you've already purchased, we are happy to use it and will adjust the quote accordingly.

Are you licensed and insured?

expand_more

Absolutely. Honolulu Paint & Drywall is fully licensed, bonded, and insured. We prioritize your peace of mind and the safety of your property above all else.

Still have questions?

We're always here to help. Reach out directly to discuss your specific needs or to schedule your free on-site consultation.
